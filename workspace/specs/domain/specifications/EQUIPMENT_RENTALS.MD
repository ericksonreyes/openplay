# Equipment rentals

- Business Owners manage the rentable-equipment catalog, activity associations, and prices; managers and staff maintain stock availability and rentals within the Business Owner's settings.
- Inventory is tracked by quantity by default. A venue may identify individual items when it needs item-level tracking.
- Players can rent equipment as an add-on to a private reservation or scheduled event, or make a standalone rental for a selected time period.
- Each rental reserves the requested quantity for its rental interval, including venue-configured pickup and return windows.
- Business Owners can configure rental durations, extensions, deposits, and late-return, damage, or loss charges for their venues. The applicable terms must be shown before booking; OpenPlay sets no platform-wide fee or deposit amount.
- The system must prevent overlapping rentals from exceeding the available stock, including when requests arrive concurrently.
- Venue staff can record equipment checkout and return and update its availability.
- Rental charges are itemized and use the same supported payment methods and payment-verification flows as other bookings.
