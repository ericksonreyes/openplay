# Facility Use Rules

Facility Use Rules are the practical instructions for using a venue or bookable resource. They are separate from system settings such as hours, prices, capacity, cancellation deadlines, and payment rules, which are defined in their own specifications.

## Ownership and scope

- Business Owners can create, edit, publish, and unpublish facility rules for their business and venues.
- A rule can apply to a business, a venue, an activity, or a specific bookable resource. The way overlapping rules take precedence remains an open decision.
- Venue managers can edit rules only when the Business Owner delegates that permission. Staff can view the rules needed to support venue operations.
- Owners can mark rules as public or internal. Internal staff instructions do not appear on public pages.
- When a rule changes booking behavior, the owner must use the related structured setting as well as any plain-language explanation. A text rule alone does not change availability, capacity, price, or payment behavior.

## Player visibility

- Public rules can appear on the Business Profile Page and venue or resource details.
- Show the rules that apply before the player submits a private reservation, event registration, or rental.
- Keep internal notes, staff instructions, and private customer or payment details out of public rule displays.
- Whether selected rules require explicit player acknowledgment, and how that acknowledgment is recorded, remains an open decision.

Examples include footwear and attire, arrival and check-in, food and drink, spectator access, equipment use, age or supervision expectations, safety, and cleanup. These examples do not impose a single rule on every venue or sport.

Facility Use Rules are operational house rules. Legal waivers and formal compliance requirements are defined separately in [Waivers and Consent](WAIVERS_AND_CONSENT.MD).
