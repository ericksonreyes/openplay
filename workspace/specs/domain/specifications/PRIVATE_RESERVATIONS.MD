# Private reservations

- Players use form-based search and inquiry by default to find venues, resources, dates, and available times.
- Players can discover a venue and its bookable resources.
- Players can view availability and create a private reservation for a selected available time.
- The system must prevent conflicting reservations for the same unavailable resource.
- Business Owners, venue managers, and delegated staff can review and manage reservations according to their permissions.
- Each venue can configure its cancellation, change, no-show, and applicable refund policies.
