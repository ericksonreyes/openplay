# Private reservations

- Players use form-based search and inquiry by default to find venues, resources, dates, and available times.
- Players can discover a venue and its bookable resources.
- A private reservation books a resource directly; event registration uses the event's participant capacity and schedule.
- Players can view availability and create a private reservation for a selected available time.
- The system must prevent conflicting reservations for the same unavailable resource.
- Business Owners, venue managers, and delegated staff can review and manage reservations according to their permissions.

## Cancellation and refunds for paid reservations

- If a player cancels at least 24 hours before the reservation starts, the default is a full refund of the amount paid.
- If a player cancels less than 24 hours before the reservation starts or does not show up, the default is no refund.
- If the venue cancels a paid reservation, the default is a full refund of the amount paid.
- Business Owners can set a different cancellation or refund policy for their venues and configure reservation changes and no-show handling. The app shows the applicable policy before payment.
- This policy does not add a no-show fee to an unpaid pay-at-venue reservation. Any such fee must be configured and disclosed separately.
