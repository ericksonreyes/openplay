# Business Profile Pages

A Business Profile Page is a public landing page for a sports business and its venues. It helps players learn what the business offers and move into OpenPlay's discovery, event, and booking flows.

## Initial profile page

- A Business Owner can create, edit, publish, and unpublish a profile for their business. In a single-company installation, the profile represents that company; in multi-tenant mode, it represents that Business Owner's business only.
- A multi-tenant Business Owner can publish profile changes after the business passes its activation review; each profile edit does not require a new Platform Administrator review.
- The page uses a standard responsive layout and can show the business name, description, logo or images, sports and activities, linked venues, published events, public hours, published Facility Use Rules, selected contact details, and links to search or book.
- Business Owners choose which contact and policy details are public. The page must not expose private bookings, guest contact details, payment records, internal notes, or unpublished content.
- Published links lead to the existing venue, event, and booking flows. Guest booking is available only where the Business Owner enables it.
- Venue managers and staff can edit profile information only when the Business Owner delegates that permission.
- The initial page is a hosted business profile, not a full website builder. Custom domains, custom page layouts, and a general-purpose content management system are not included.

## Open profile decisions

Exact profile fields, brand assets, public contact choices, and delegated edit permissions remain to be defined. Business activation is reviewed once; active Business Owners publish profile edits. Slug pattern, collision behavior, redirect window, metadata, structured data, and what is indexable live in [Public Content Delivery](../../technical/PUBLIC_CONTENT_DELIVERY.MD).
