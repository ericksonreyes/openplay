# Disputes

A dispute is a structured way for a player or guest to raise a problem about a booking, event, rental, or payment — for example a charge they believe is wrong, a no-show marking they disagree with, or an equipment return the venue claims is missing.

## Opening a dispute

- A player or verified guest can open a dispute on one of their own bookings, registrations, rentals, or payments.
- The dispute names the affected record, a category (payment, no-show, cancellation, equipment, service), and the player's account of what happened. Supporting files such as receipt images can be attached.
- A dispute is open for a limited window after the event — the exact deadline is venue- or platform-configurable.

## Handling a dispute

- The venue sees open disputes in its operations view and responds through the app; venue managers and delegated staff handle responses, while refund decisions stay with the Business Owner.
- A dispute has a status: open, under review, resolved, or escalated. Each response and status change is recorded so both sides see the same history.
- Evidence is drawn from existing records — attendance markings, payment verification records, and the audit log — rather than new ad-hoc claims.
- Opening a dispute does not change payment, booking, or rental status by itself. A charge remains recorded as charged until an authorized refund or adjustment happens through the payment flow.
- Guests raise disputes through the same flow after completing booking verification.

## Escalation and records

- In a multi-tenant deployment, a player may escalate an unresolved dispute to the Platform Administrator, whose involvement is logged like other cross-business access.
- Disputes and their resolution feed venue reporting and the audit trail.
- Notifications keep both sides informed of status changes; see [Notifications](NOTIFICATIONS.MD).

## Open decisions

- Response-time expectations for venues and escalation timing to the platform.
- Whether a dispute can pause collection actions (for example a pending no-show fee) while open.
- Which categories route to the Business Owner versus a manager by default.
- Whether disputes can block a business's public profile or payouts in serious cases.
- Guest dispute evidence when no account exists (verified contact records).
- Whether players can withdraw a dispute and whether reopening is allowed.
