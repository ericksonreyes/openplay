# Product goals

- Give players a consistent way to discover businesses and activities, reserve a resource, register for scheduled events such as Open Play, and receive timely booking updates.
- Give venues a flexible way to manage bookable resources, availability, reservations, scheduled events and registrations, rentable equipment, membership plans, public Business Profile Pages, and Facility Use Rules.
- Keep common booking and capacity behavior independent of any one sport or activity.
- Help players discover a business's venues and events through a public profile page.
- Support multiple venues without treating their resources and operations as one venue.
