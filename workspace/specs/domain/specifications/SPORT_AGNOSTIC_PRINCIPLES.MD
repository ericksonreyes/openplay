# Sport-agnostic product principles

- Treat venues, resources, availability, reservations, scheduled events, and event capacity as shared concepts.
- Allow activities to be identified or configured without making their specialized rules mandatory for the common booking flow.
- Do not require pickleball-specific ratings, equipment, or terminology for a venue to use the core reservation features.
- Keep future sport- or activity-specific capabilities optional so they do not constrain other use cases.
- Rentable equipment can be associated with an activity without making the rental flow dependent on pickleball-specific equipment or rules.
