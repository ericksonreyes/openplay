# Waivers and consent

A waiver is a legal acknowledgment a player accepts before participating — for example a liability release for court play. Waivers are separate from [Facility Use Rules](FACILITY_USE_RULES.MD): house rules are operational instructions; a waiver is a recorded legal consent.

## Defining waivers

- Business Owners can create, edit, publish, and retire waiver documents for their business, a venue, an activity, or a bookable resource.
- A waiver has versioned text. Changing the text creates a new version; previously signed versions are kept for the record.
- Venue managers can edit waivers only when the Business Owner delegates that permission.
- Whether a waiver is required before a booking, a check-in, or a first visit is venue-configurable.

## Recording consent

- When a waiver is required, the player must acknowledge it before the protected action completes — for example before submitting a reservation or at check-in.
- Each consent records the player or guest, the waiver version, the timestamp, and what action it covered. Consent is auditable and cannot be edited after recording.
- A new waiver version does not retroactively cover earlier bookings; whether it must be re-acknowledged for future bookings is configurable.
- Guests acknowledge waivers through the same flow, recorded against their verified contact reference.

## Minors and guardians

- A participant who is a minor needs consent from a parent or guardian. The guardian acknowledges the waiver on the participant's behalf, and the record shows both the participant and the consenting guardian.
- A signed-in player can register a minor participant under their account or booking when the venue allows it; the adult account holder is treated as the consenting guardian.
- Age thresholds and whether a venue requires guardian presence at check-in are venue-configurable rules.

## Open decisions

- The acknowledgment method (checkbox with timestamped record vs. a drawn signature).
- Whether consent is per booking, per defined period, or once per waiver version.
- Exact age threshold for requiring a guardian, and how guardian identity is verified.
- How long waiver documents and consent records are retained.
- Whether waivers can be required for event registration and equipment rental as well as reservations.
- Multi-language waiver versions.
