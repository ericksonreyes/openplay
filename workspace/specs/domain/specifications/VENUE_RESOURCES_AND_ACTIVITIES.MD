# Venue resources and activities

- A venue can publish bookable spaces or resources.
- Resources can be associated with the sports or activities offered at that venue.
- The shared reservation flow must not require pickleball-specific data or rules; activity-specific configuration should be optional.
- Business Owners configure operating hours, reservation durations, booking start-time increments, buffers, booking horizons, and venue policies; managers maintain day-to-day schedules and availability within those settings.
- Court reservations have a default 10-minute turnover buffer after the scheduled end time; Business Owners can override it, including setting it to zero. Buffers for other resource types are venue-configurable.
- The booking start-time grid is separate from the turnover buffer and is venue-configurable.
- Business Owners set standard prices for resources and sessions; managers do not change prices by default.
- Venue-specific values can override platform defaults; no single setting is imposed on every venue.
