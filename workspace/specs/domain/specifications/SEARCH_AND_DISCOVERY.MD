# Search and discovery

Players can discover published Business Profile Pages, venues, bookable resources, activities, and scheduled events through form-based search. Public browsing does not require an account.

## Initial search behavior

- Players can search by activity or sport, a location they enter, date and time, offering type, and published availability. Device location is offered only after the player explicitly opts in.
- The initial release includes an interactive map and radius filters alongside the result list. Radius uses the player's local distance units, and the player chooses a radius before filtering.
- Search can include private reservation options, scheduled events such as Open Play, and public business or venue pages.
- When available, results show the public business and venue details, applicable activity, time or event schedule, price, and remaining event capacity.
- Results include a clear path to review details and continue to the normal reservation or event registration form. Search does not hold a resource or event place.
- Search results include only published businesses, venues, resources, and events that are open for discovery. Private booking details and guest contact information never appear in search.
- Availability in a result is informational. The booking or registration flow rechecks current availability and capacity before it confirms the request.
- Results are ordered by relevance to the player's selected activity, location, time, and offering type; exact signal weights and tie-break behavior remain open.
- Form-based search is the default and remains available when the optional AI assistant is disabled.

## Open discovery decisions

Exact radius preset values and relevance weights/tie-break behavior, price filters, saved searches, and supported resource attributes remain to be decided.
