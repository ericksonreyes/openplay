# Platforms and payments

- Player and venue workflows are planned for a responsive, mobile-first web app that works in desktop and mobile browsers. Native iOS and Android apps are deferred to a later phase.
- Planned payment options include GCash and Maya through integrated checkout or direct transfer to a venue account, as well as paying at the venue. Direct e-wallet or bank transfers allow venues to accept funds even when they do not use a payment gateway.
- Business Owners choose which supported payment methods to offer and provide any required transfer instructions or account details.
- Membership purchases use the supported payment methods and verification flows; plans may be one-time or recurring where the payment method supports repeat charges. Direct transfers and pay-at-venue renewals are manual unless a recurring-payment flow is configured. Benefit activation follows the membership plan's policy.
- Business Owners configure whether reservations, event registrations, and rentals require full payment, a deposit, or later payment where allowed, and when any remaining balance is due. See [Scheduled Events](SCHEDULED_EVENTS.MD) for event entry-fee rules.
- Paid private reservation cancellations and refunds follow the policy in [Private Reservations](PRIVATE_RESERVATIONS.MD).
- Booking confirmation and payment status are separate: a reservation, event registration, or equipment rental can be confirmed while payment remains due.
- For reservations or event registrations with payment due at check-in, standalone rentals with payment due at pickup, and balances due after a deposit, the default payment grace period is 15 minutes after the scheduled start or pickup time; Business Owners can configure a different duration.

## Booking confirmation and payment flows

- Instant booking is the default when a resource is available. A venue can enable request-based approval separately from its choice of payment method.
- For integrated checkout, the system temporarily holds the relevant resource, event capacity, or equipment quantity while the player completes payment. It confirms the reservation, event registration, or rental only after successful payment is verified server-side through the provider; a browser redirect alone is not proof of payment. Failed, cancelled, or expired checkout releases the hold.
- For direct transfers, the system temporarily holds the relevant resource, event capacity, or equipment quantity and marks the payment as awaiting venue verification. The player can submit a transaction reference and optionally a receipt image. Authorized venue staff verify the incoming transfer against the venue's official wallet or bank account records. A screenshot alone does not confirm payment. A verified transfer confirms the reservation, event registration, or rental and marks it paid; rejection or hold expiry releases the hold.
- For pay-at-venue, a reservation, event registration, or rental can be confirmed with payment recorded as due at the venue or equipment pickup. Staff record payment when it is collected.
- A confirmed reservation, event registration, or rental with an unpaid amount remains confirmed, and its resource, event capacity, or equipment quantity remains allocated during its grace period. If payment is still outstanding when the grace period ends, the venue's overdue, cancellation, or no-show policy applies. Once a resource's scheduled use has started, it must not be reopened for rebooking. Equipment already checked out remains unavailable until staff record its return; uncollected equipment can be released according to the venue's no-show policy.
- If a venue requires approval for a reservation, event registration, or rental, the request remains pending and holds the requested resource, event capacity, or equipment quantity for a limited period; no payment is collected while approval is pending. After approval, the selected payment flow begins, or the reservation, event registration, or rental is confirmed with payment due at the venue or equipment pickup. Rejected or expired requests release the hold.
