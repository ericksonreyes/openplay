# Product overview

OpenPlay is a sport-agnostic platform concept for discovering and reserving courts and other activity spaces. Pickleball is the starting use case, while the reservation core is intended to support other sports and bookable activities without depending on sport-specific rules.

The product serves players looking for activities and venues managing their spaces. It is planned to support multiple venues, two activity types, equipment rentals, and venue membership plans:

- **Private reservations:** A player books a resource, such as a court or room, for a selected time.
- **Open-play sessions:** A player joins a scheduled session that has a participant limit.
- **Equipment rentals:** A player rents equipment with a reservation or session, or separately for a selected time period.
- **Memberships:** A player can purchase a venue-specific plan with defined eligibility and benefits.

Players use form-based search and inquiry as the default way to find availability and ask about venue offerings. The optional AI chatbot can supplement this path but does not replace it.
