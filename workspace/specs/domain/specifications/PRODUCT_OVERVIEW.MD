# Product overview

OpenPlay is a sport-agnostic platform concept for discovering and reserving courts and other activity spaces. Pickleball is the starting use case, while the reservation core is intended to support other sports and bookable activities without depending on sport-specific rules.

The product serves players looking for activities and Businesses managing their venues. It is planned to support private reservations, scheduled events, equipment rentals, memberships that can cover one or more venues, and public Business Profile Pages:

- **Private reservations:** A player books a resource, such as a court or room, for a selected time.
- **Scheduled events:** Players register for organized activities at a venue. Open Play is the first defined event format.
- **Equipment rentals:** A player rents equipment with a reservation or event, or separately for a selected time period.
- **Memberships:** A player can purchase a plan that applies to selected venues or all venues in a business, with defined eligibility and benefits.
- **Business Profile Pages:** A Business Owner publishes public information about the business, its venues, its offerings, and selected Facility Use Rules.

Players use form-based search and inquiry as the default way to find availability and ask about venue offerings. OpenPlay sends transactional updates for important booking, event, and payment changes. The optional AI chatbot can supplement search but does not replace forms.
