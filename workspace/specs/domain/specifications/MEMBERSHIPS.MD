# Memberships

- Membership plans are specific to a venue; Business Owners create and update plan eligibility, price, term, and benefits, such as eligible booking or rental discounts or an earlier booking window.
- Managers administer membership operations within the Business Owner's plan rules.
- Players can view available plans, purchase a membership, and see its status and benefits.
- The system applies a member's active benefits to eligible reservations, open-play sessions, or equipment rentals.
- A membership represents access and benefits, not an in-app cash balance or stored-value wallet.
